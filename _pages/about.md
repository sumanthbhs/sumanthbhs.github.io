I am **Dr. Sumanth Bharadwaj H. S.**, an Assistant Professor in the Department of Mathematics and
Statistics at [M. S. Ramaiah University of Applied Sciences](https://www.msruas.ac.in/), Bengaluru.
I completed my Ph.D. in 2017 at Bangalore University under the supervision of
Prof. M. S. Mahadeva Naika.

My research is in **number theory** and **partition theory**, with a focus on **Ramanujan's
theta-function identities** and congruences for partition functions — including regular
overpartitions, colour partitions, and designated summands. Alongside research, I teach a broad
range of undergraduate and postgraduate mathematics courses and enjoy building interactive
visualizations of mathematical ideas.

You can browse my [publications](/publications/) and [CV](/cv/), or find my work on
[Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=en&user=itdKlOoAAAAJ). The links below are
the best ways to reach me.
